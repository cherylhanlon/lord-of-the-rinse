The practice

A short tale about a dental practice

We are a modern, independent practice in Gosforth. We see nervous patients, curious patients, and patients who have not been near a dentist since the last age.

Why the silly name

Because dentistry has quite enough solemnity already. The joke on the door is deliberate: it is the first signal that nobody here is going to lecture you, and that asking a question will never feel like an imposition.

Behind it is a practice that takes the clinical side entirely seriously — full examinations, our own CBCT scanner for implant and surgical planning, and treatment options explained with the costs attached before you commit to anything.

All we have to decide is what to do with the teeth that are given us.
— The practice motto, more or less

What to expect first visit

  • A full adult examination, unhurried
  • Any x-rays we need, taken and explained on the spot
  • A written plan with fees, before you decide
  • Plan membership offered, never pushed

How we work

Four habits that shape every appointment here.

The first

Prevention before repair

Hygiene visits and honest advice cost a fraction of the treatment they head off, so that is where we start.

The second

Fees in the open

Our whole price list is published. You will never learn the cost of something at the desk on the way out.

The third

Options, not instructions

Where there is more than one sensible route, you get all of them — including doing nothing for now.

The fourth

Time for nervous patients

Longer appointments and sedation where it is suitable. Nobody is hurried through a door here.

The practice, in numbers

Six days a week, open until seven, with five membership plans covering everyone from a student on a budget to a household of six.
