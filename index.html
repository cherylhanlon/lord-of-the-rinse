What we do here

Prevention first, restoration when it is needed, and a straight answer about which is which.

Chapter I

Examinations

A full adult examination with oral health assessment, from £95 — or included on your plan.

Chapter II

Hygiene & prevention

Twenty- and forty-minute hygiene appointments, stain removal and advice that actually fits your routine.

Chapter III

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, tooth-coloured fillings and crowns, or a complete smile makeover planned from scratch.

Chapter IV

Implants

Single implants, implant-retained dentures and full-arch rehabilitation, planned on our own CBCT scanner.

Chapter V

Orthodontics

Metal, ceramic and lingual brace systems, starting with a full assessment and OPG.

Chapter VI

Nervous patients

Sedation is available where it is suitable, and nobody here will rush you into a decision.

A practice that explains itself

Lord of the Rinse Dental grew out of a simple frustration: dental care that was never quite explained, and prices you only discovered at the desk on the way out.

So we publish our fees, we talk through the options before anything is booked, and we keep the plans simple enough to compare on one page. The name is a joke. The dentistry is not.
