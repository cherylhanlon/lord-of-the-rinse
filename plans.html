The small print, in plain words

Nothing here is designed to catch you out.

On paying

Monthly Direct Debit

Your fee is collected monthly. Joining fees, where they apply, are taken with the first payment.

On leaving

Cancel after the term

Minimum terms are six or twelve months depending on the plan. After that, cancel whenever you wish.

On using it

Entitlements run yearly

Check-ups and hygiene visits are allocated per plan year. Unused visits do not roll forward.

On discounts

Applied at the desk

Your treatment discount comes off the published fee automatically — you never have to ask for it.
